## Fix applied

libredesk has a strict koanf config schema and stacker does not interpolate
every service-level env var. Fixed by shipping a complete `config.toml`
bind-mounted to `/libredesk/config.toml`, generated by
`scripts/generate-secrets.sh` with the real DB password and a required
`app.encryption_key` (openssl rand -hex 16). The full sample config (all
required sections: autoassigner, message, notification, automation, ai_agent,
webhook, ssrf, conversation, sla, rate_limit.*) is written at pre_build.
